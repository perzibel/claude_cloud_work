# Virality Predictor rubric (0-100)

| Dimension | Wt | What earns full marks |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (first 1-3s) | 25 | Strong visual/sound motion at frame 0, no intro, instantly clear "why watch"; text hook reinforces |
| Retention & loop | 20 | Tight length (15-30s), pacing change every 2-4s, payoff before the end, seamless loop/rewatch trigger |
| Emotion & shareability | 15 | Strong emotion, relatable lyric, "send this to someone" or "save for later" quality, surprise |
| Audio trend-fit | 15 | Recognisable hook, catchy 15s section, fits current sound/dance/edit trends, usable as a sound |
| Visual & format quality | 10 | 9:16, sharp, well lit, subject centred in safe zone, clean audio levels, captions legible |
| Caption & CTA | 10 | Hook text <=8 words, clear single CTA, comment-bait question or opinion |
| Discoverability | 5 | Relevant niche hashtags/keywords, collab tag, strong cover frame |

Bands: 85+ post immediately; 70-84 post, minor tweaks; 55-69 re-cut/re-hook; <55 pick another moment.

Output per candidate: score, per-dimension scores, top weakness, one concrete fix.
Penalties (-5 to -15): slow intro, mid-word cut, watermark from other platforms,
text in UI overlap zone, low-res, silence at start, uncleared content.
